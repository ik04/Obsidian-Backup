## Persistence:
- don't take failure as an answer
- keep persisting till you get your desired goal
- i felt this with dev 
- never give up 

## Self Talk:
- stop being negative to yourself
- you're the only person supporting yourself
- there's enough people in the world to tell you the things you tell yourself 
- be kind to yourself and be a cheerleader, be the captain of your own ship 
- write about all the things who make you who you are **(write it when you're good, when you're in a good mood)****
- get rid of people who bring you down, rip them out of your life

## Build other people's self confidence:
- when doing don't bring them down on their faults
- uplift people when they do well
- "catch them when they're good"
- acknowledge faults as something normal
