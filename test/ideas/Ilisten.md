## Concept:
- easier way to write diaries, records audio and stores it in a server, allows you to perform text to speech on the audio and read the transcript.
- Performs sentiment analyses on the transcript to determine the emotion felt/senitment analysis on the voice to determine the sentiment
- allows you listen back on stored audio 
- #futureFeature allows emulation of your voice to say things to you (inspired by akshit)
